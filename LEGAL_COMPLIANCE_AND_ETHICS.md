# Legal Compliance, Ethical Boundaries & Regulatory QA Framework

This document defines the mandatory legal, ethical, data-protection, and regulatory-quality rules governing the use of this software and its generated deliverables across **Switzerland** and the **European Union**.

---

## 1. Cybersecurity & Passive External Observation (`StGB Art. 143bis` & NCSC Switzerland)

1. **Strictly Passive Observation Only**:
   - All domain compliance and security checks in this toolkit operate **exclusively on publicly broadcast metadata** identical to a standard web browser visit (`HTTP/HTTPS GET` response headers, initial public HTML DOM, standard TLS certificate handshake, and public DNS `TXT`/`MX`/`CAA` records via DNS-over-HTTPS).
   - Under **Article 143bis of the Swiss Criminal Code (*Strafgesetzbuch*, StGB)** (*Unbefugtes Eindringen in ein Datenverarbeitungssystem*), criminal liability applies to bypassing security barriers or intruding into a protected system.
   - **Prohibited Actions**: Users of this repository must **NEVER** perform active port scanning, directory brute-forcing, SQL/XSS payload injection, fuzzing, rate-limit exhaustion, or authentication circumvention against any domain without a signed written penetration-testing mandate from the domain owner.
2. **Confidentiality of Third-Party Findings**:
   - Audit packages generated for prospective or active B2B clients must be stored privately (`output/` and `live_targets_output/` are git-ignored by default) and transmitted **solely to the authorized domain owner**.
   - Never publish a real third-party organization's security or privacy gaps in a public repository; use only synthetic demonstration domains (e.g., `alpine-treuhand-demo.ch` or `healthtech-demo.ch`) in public showcases.

## 2. Swiss & EU B2B Outreach Compliance (`Swiss UWG Art. 3(1)(o)`, `nFADP`, `EU GDPR` & `CNIL`)

1. **No Automated Bulk Spam (`Swiss UWG Art. 3 Abs. 1 lit. o`)**:
   - Switzerland’s Federal Act against Unfair Competition (**UWG / LCD Art. 3 Abs. 1 lit. o**) prohibits automated mass commercial advertising (*Massenwerbung*) without prior consent, including in B2B contexts.
   - Accordingly, this toolkit is **not** a bulk email blaster. Any B2B outreach must be conducted as **individually researched, 1-to-1 professional correspondence** sent manually to a company's published corporate contact point regarding a specific, verified technical or regulatory need relevant to their business role.
2. **EU / French B2B Legitimate Interest (`GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)` & `CNIL` B2B Guidelines)**:
   - In EU jurisdictions (such as France and Germany), B2B technical outreach to corporate addresses (`info@`, `contact@`, or a professional's work email) is lawful under `GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)` provided the subject matter is directly relevant to the recipient's professional function, the sender's full identity and contact details are transparent, and an immediate, frictionless opt-out is respected.
3. **Mandatory Elements in Every Outreach Message**:
   - Clear identification of the sender (full name, location, and link to methodology).
   - Zero deceptive subject lines or false urgency claims.
   - A simple 1-sentence opt-out/no-follow-up assurance (e.g., *"If this is not relevant to your team, simply let me know and I will not follow up."*).

## 3. Medical Device & Regulatory Writing QA Disclaimer (`EU MDR 2017/745`, `Swiss MedDO` & `ALCOA+`)

1. **Human-in-the-Loop Verification Required**:
   - All PRISMA 2020 flowcharts, Vancouver clinical evidence tables, OpenFDA MAUDE adverse-event summaries, and `ClinicalTrials.gov` surveillance workbooks generated by this software are **automated pre-screening and formatting drafts**.
   - In accordance with **EU MDR 2017/745 (Annex III, Annex XIV, and Article 15 PRRC)**, **MDCG 2020-6 / MDCG 2022-21**, and **ALCOA+ data integrity principles**, every extracted conclusion, exclusion reason, and adverse-event count **must be reviewed and signed off by a qualified medical writer or regulatory affairs specialist** against the full-text primary publications before submission to a Notified Body or Swissmedic.
2. **Zero Fabrication Integrity Rule**:
   - The software never invents DOIs, PMIDs, NCT IDs, or study conclusions. Any record missing a required field is automatically highlighted in soft amber and flagged with `Needs verification` in the exported Excel workbook.
3. **No Legal or Medical Advice**:
   - The outputs of this software constitute technical data-engineering and formatting support; they do not constitute formal legal representation (*Rechtsberatung*) or clinical medical advice.
