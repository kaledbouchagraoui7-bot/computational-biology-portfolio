### 1. UNSOLICITED HEALTHCARE PROVIDER (HCP) INQUIRY
*"What is the incidence of immune-mediated pneumonitis and hypothyroidism observed in NCT03719066 for Triple-Negative Breast Cancer?"*

### 2. PICO LITERATURE SEARCH STRATEGY
- **Population:** Adults with untreated locally recurrent inoperable or metastatic Triple-Negative Breast Cancer (N=847).
- **Intervention / Comparator:** Monoclonal antibody + chemotherapy (N=422) vs. placebo + chemotherapy (N=425).
- **Outcome:** Incidence of Grade >= 3 TEAEs (48.2% vs. 43.1%), hypothyroidism (14.2%), and pneumonitis (3.1%).

### 3. SCIENTIFIC RESPONSE SUMMARY
In NCT03719066, immune-mediated adverse events occurred in 26.5% of patients in the experimental arm, with hypothyroidism in 14.2% and pneumonitis in 3.1%. Discontinuation due to AEs was 8.3% vs. 5.1%.
