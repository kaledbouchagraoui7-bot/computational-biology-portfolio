### 1. NON-CLINICAL & CLINICAL PHARMACOLOGY SUMMARY
- **Mechanism:** Checkpoint blockade restoring T-cell-mediated anti-tumour immunity in Triple-Negative Breast Cancer.
- **Reference Trial:** NCT03719066 (N=847: Experimental N=422, Control N=425).

### 2. CUMULATIVE CLINICAL EFFICACY & SAFETY GUIDANCE
- Median PFS improved from 5.6 months to 9.7 months (HR 0.65 (95% CI: 0.55 - 0.77), p < 0.0001).
- Grade >= 3 TEAEs occurred in 48.2% vs. 43.1%; investigators must monitor thyroid function (14.2% hypothyroidism) and respiratory symptoms (3.1% pneumonitis).
