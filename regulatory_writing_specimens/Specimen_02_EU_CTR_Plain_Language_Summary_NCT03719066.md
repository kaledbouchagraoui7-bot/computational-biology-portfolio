# PLAIN LANGUAGE SUMMARY OF CLINICAL TRIAL RESULTS

> **Regulatory Compliance:** Prepared in accordance with EU Clinical Trials Regulation (EU CTR 536/2014 Annex V) and Good Lay Summary Practice (GLSP). Informational portfolio specimen; non-promotional and neutral in tone.

### 1. WHAT WAS THE MAIN QUESTION?
Doctors wanted to learn if adding a new study medicine to standard chemotherapy helps people with Triple-Negative Breast Cancer live longer without their cancer growing.

### 2. WHO TOOK PART?
- **Total People:** 847 adults across 26 countries.
- **New Treatment Group:** 422 people received the study medicine plus chemotherapy.
- **Comparison Group:** 425 people received a dummy medicine (placebo) plus chemotherapy.

### 3. WHAT WERE THE RESULTS?
- **Time Without Cancer Growth:** People in the new treatment group went **9.7 months** on average before cancer grew, compared with **5.6 months** in the comparison group.
- **Overall Survival:** Half of the people in the new treatment group lived at least **23.0 months**, compared with **16.1 months** in the comparison group.
- **Tumour Shrinkage:** About **53%** (52.7%) of people in the new treatment group saw their tumours shrink, compared with **41%** (40.8%) in the comparison group.

### 4. WHAT SIDE EFFECTS HAPPENED?
- **Serious Side Effects (Grade 3 or higher):** Happened in **48%** (48.2%) of people in the new treatment group and **43%** (43.1%) in the comparison group.
- **Immune-Related Side Effects:** Seen in **27%** (26.5%) of people taking the study medicine, including low thyroid hormone in **14%** (14.2%) and lung inflammation in **3%** (3.1%).
- **Stopping Treatment Early:** **8.3%** stopped because of side effects in the new treatment group vs. **5.1%** in the comparison group.
