### 2. STUDY OBJECTIVES & METHODOLOGY
- **Primary Objective:** Evaluate progression-free survival (PFS) per RECIST v1.1 by blinded independent central review (BICR).
- **Randomization:** 1:1 (Experimental Arm N=422 vs. Control Arm N=425), total N=847.

### 3. EFFICACY RESULTS
| Efficacy Parameter | Experimental Arm (N=422) | Control Arm (N=425) | Effect Estimate (95% CI) | p-value |
|:---|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| **Median PFS (BICR)** | **9.7 months** | **5.6 months** | **HR 0.65 (95% CI: 0.55 - 0.77)** | **p < 0.0001** |
| **Median OS (Interim)** | **23.0 months** | **16.1 months** | **HR 0.73 (95% CI: 0.62 - 0.86)** | **p = 0.0012** |
| **Objective Response Rate (ORR)** | **52.7%** | **40.8%** | **OR 1.61 (95% CI: 1.23 - 2.12)** | **p = 0.0005** |

### 4. SAFETY PROFILE
- Grade >= 3 TEAEs: **48.2%** (Experimental) vs. **43.1%** (Control).
- Immune-mediated adverse events (imAEs): **26.5%** (hypothyroidism **14.2%**, pneumonitis **3.1%**).
- Treatment discontinuation due to AEs: **8.3%** (Experimental) vs. **5.1%** (Control).
