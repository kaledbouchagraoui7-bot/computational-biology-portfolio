### 1. PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT RATIONALE & OVERVIEW OF EFFICACY
- **Indication:** Unresectable locally recurrent or metastatic Triple-Negative Breast Cancer (pivotal trial NCT03719066, N=847).
- **Efficacy Summary:** Median PFS improved from 5.6 months (N=425) to 9.7 months (N=422), HR 0.65 (95% CI: 0.55 - 0.77) (p < 0.0001). Interim median OS improved from 16.1 months to 23.0 months, HR 0.73 (95% CI: 0.62 - 0.86) (p = 0.0012). ORR was 52.7% vs. 40.8% (OR 1.61 (95% CI: 1.23 - 2.12), p = 0.0005).

### 2. OVERVIEW OF SAFETY & BENEFIT-RISK CONCLUSION
- Grade >= 3 TEAEs occurred in 48.2% vs. 43.1%, with discontinuations in 8.3% vs. 5.1%.
- Overall benefit-risk balance is positive for the target population.
