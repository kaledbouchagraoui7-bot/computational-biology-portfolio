## 2. Executive Summary — 5 Most Recent High-Impact Findings

- **PMID [42960330](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42960330/)** (2026 | *Saudi journal of anaesthesia* | `Review`):  
  **Perioperative management of GLP-1 receptor agonists and SGLT2 inhibitors: An updated review for anesthesiologists.**  
  *Key Regulatory Finding:* **The review highlights the central role of anesthesiologists in coordinating multidisciplinary, patient-centered care that balances metabolic safety with preservation of established cardiovascular and renal benefits.** [[DOI]](https://doi.org/10.4103/sja.sja_127_26)
- **PMID [42959190](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42959190/)** (2026 | *Clinical kidney journal* | `Review`):  
  **Single or multiple nutritional hormone receptor modulators for chronic kidney disease.**  
  *Key Regulatory Finding:* **Ongoing mechanistic studies and dedicated outcome trials will further define the role of incretin biology in the prevention and treatment of CKD.** [[DOI]](https://doi.org/10.1093/ckj/sfag311)
- **PMID [42953658](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42953658/)** (2026 | *Frontiers in medicine* | `Review`):  
  **Research progress on bowel preparation regimens before colonoscopy: a narrative review.**  
  *Key Regulatory Finding:* **The review concludes that no single regimen is universally optimal; personalized preparation strategies integrating risk stratification, AI-assisted quality assessment, and evidence-based adjunctive protocols represent the most promising path toward improving colonoscopy outcomes at the population level.** [[DOI]](https://doi.org/10.3389/fmed.2026.1959875)
- **PMID [42952754](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42952754/)** (2026 | *Frontiers in endocrinology* | `Network Meta-Analysis; Systematic Review`):  
  **Network and pairwise meta-analysis of the effects of novel hypoglycemic agents on cardiorenal outcomes in patients with type 2 diabetes and heart failure.**  
  *Key Regulatory Finding:* **Optimal clinical management of T2DM requires a comprehensive assessment of cardiovascular and renal comorbidities. This network meta-analysis indicates that SGLT-2is significantly reduce the risk of HF-related composite endpoints and CV death in T2DM patients with HF, although their renoprotective effect does not reach statistical significance. GLP-1RAs also demonstrate beneficial effects on HF-related composite endpoints. These findings suggest that both SGLT-2is and GLP-1RAs are preferred glycemic control strategies for improving cardiovascular outcomes in T2DM patients with HF, while further evidence is needed to confirm their definitive renal benefits.** [[DOI]](https://doi.org/10.3389/fendo.2026.1955431)
- **PMID [42949133](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42949133/)** (2026 | *Journal of diabetes science and technology* | `Clinical Investigation`):  
  **Multisystem Outcomes of GLP-1 Receptor Agonists Versus Active Comparators in Type 2 Diabetes: A Multicenter Target Trial Emulation.**  
  *Key Regulatory Finding:* **Glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonist initiation was associated with reduced cardiovascular, renal, hematologic, and tobacco-related risk, consistent with outcome trials. Increased risks for musculoskeletal, gastrointestinal, and sleep-wake disorders warrant monitoring as the clinical use of GLP-1RAs continues to expand.** [[DOI]](https://doi.org/10.1177/19322968261498914)
